# Rainbow subgraphs: obstructions, progress and open problems

Website of the survey *Rainbow subgraphs: obstructions, progress and open problems*: a reading version of the full text with figures and linked references, a searchable list of all theorems, conjectures and problems, the status of every open problem, a timeline of the major conjectures, the threshold gaps and a map of the translations from other areas.

Site: https://threelu.github.io/rainbow-subgraphs/

The pages are generated from the LaTeX source of the survey; the numbering of sections, statements and figures agrees with the PDF (`survey.pdf`).

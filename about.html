About

This site is the online version of the survey Rainbow subgraphs: obstructions, progress and open problems. The reading pages, the statements, the history and the translations are all generated from the LaTeX source of the survey and the data of its appendix, so they agree with the PDF, including the numbering of sections, statements and figures.

Scope

The survey covers rainbow matchings and transversals, spanning trees and matroid bases, paths and cycles, general spanning structures, edge-coloured digraphs, and hypergraphs, designs and set families. Graph collections and random settings are treated with the corresponding structures. Anti-Ramsey numbers, rainbow Turán numbers and rainbow connection are not covered.

How to read the status of a problem

Open: no proof is known; where partial progress is stated in the text, it is noted. Proved for large n: proved for all sufficiently large orders. Proved: proved in full. Disproved: false as stated; the text describes what survives.
